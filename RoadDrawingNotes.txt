Pole Position — road drawing notes
=================================

THE TRICK
---------
The road is not one big shape. It is painted one horizontal row at a time
from the horizon down to the bottom of the screen.

Each row draws three short bars around the screen center:
  1) left curb (black)
  2) gray road
  3) right curb (black)

Rows near the horizon are narrow (far away).
Rows near the bottom are wide (close to the camera).
That creates the classic trapezoid / perspective look.


FLOW OF Draw()
--------------
1. Read the window size (viewport).
2. Figure out where the horizon is (horizonRow) from SkyHeightFraction.
3. Clear the screen, then draw the sky band and the full grass band.
4. Loop screenRow from horizonRow to the bottom of the window:
   a. depthFromHorizon = how far down this row is (0 = far, 1 = near)
   b. halfRoadWidth    = lerp far road width -> near road width
   c. curbWidth        = lerp far curb width -> near curb width
   d. Draw left curb, gray road, right curb centered on roadCenterX
5. End SpriteBatch.


VARIABLE GLOSSARY
-----------------
_pixel
  1x1 white texture used as a "paintbrush" for colored rectangles.

SkyColor / GrassColor / RoadColor / CurbColor
  Tint colors for sky, grass, road surface, and road edges.

SkyHeightFraction
  How much of the screen height is sky (0.4 = top 40%).

FarRoadHalfWidthFraction
  At the horizon: half the road width as a fraction of screen width.
  "Half" because we measure left/right from the center.

NearRoadHalfWidthFraction
  At the bottom of the screen: half the road width as a fraction
  of screen width.

FarCurbWidthPixels / NearCurbWidthPixels
  Black curb thickness in pixels at the horizon (far) and bottom (near).

horizonRow
  Y pixel where sky meets ground. Road drawing starts here.

groundRowCount
  How many rows exist from the horizon to the bottom of the screen.

roadCenterX
  Horizontal center of the road (middle of the window for a straight road).

farRoadHalfWidthPixels / nearRoadHalfWidthPixels
  Far/near half-road widths converted from fractions into actual pixels
  for this window size.

screenRow
  The current horizontal scanline (Y) being painted in the loop.

depthFromHorizon
  0.0 at the horizon (far), 1.0 at the bottom (near).
  Used to blend widths between far and near.

halfRoadWidth
  Half the gray road width on this screenRow, in pixels.

curbWidth
  Black curb width on this screenRow, in pixels.


DRAWING ONE ROW (around roadCenterX)
------------------------------------
Left curb starts at:  roadCenterX - halfRoadWidth - curbWidth
Gray road starts at:  roadCenterX - halfRoadWidth
                      width = halfRoadWidth * 2
Right curb starts at: roadCenterX + halfRoadWidth
