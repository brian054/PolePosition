Pole Position — road drawing notes
=================================

THE TRICK
---------
The road is not one big shape. It is painted one horizontal row at a time
from the horizon down to the bottom of the screen.

Each row draws up to six short bars around the screen center:
    1) left curb (black)
    2) gray road
    3) right curb (black)
    4) white center dash (only on dash rows, not gap rows)
    5) white left edge dash (same dash rows as the center)
    6) white right edge dash (same dash rows as the center)

Rows near the horizon are narrow (far away).
Rows near the bottom are wide (close to the camera).
That creates the classic trapezoid / perspective look.


SCROLL / INFINITE ROAD
----------------------
Holding Enter or Space advances _roadScroll at a fixed RoadScrollSpeed.
Releasing both keys freezes the road.

For each screen row we map depth to a world Z with:
  worldZ = 1 / max(depthFromHorizon, DepthEpsilon)

Then:
  stripePos = worldZ + _roadScroll
  phase     = stripePos % (DashLength + GapLength)

If phase is in the dash half of the period, draw the center line and
the left/right edge dashes. They share the same scroll, so they stay
locked together as the road moves.
Because worldZ packs near the horizon, dashes look denser far away
and longer up close — that sells the infinite-road motion.


FLOW OF Draw()
--------------
1. Read the window size (viewport).
2. Figure out where the horizon is (horizonRow) from SkyHeightFraction.
3. Clear the screen, then draw the sky band and the full grass band.
4. Loop screenRow from horizonRow to the bottom of the window:
   a. depthFromHorizon = how far down this row is (0 = far, 1 = near)
   b. halfRoadWidth    = lerp far road width -> near road width
   c. curbWidth        = lerp far curb width -> near curb width
   d. Draw left curb, gray road, right curb centered on roadCenterX
                  e. Map depth -> worldZ, add _roadScroll, decide dash vs gap
                  f. If dash: draw white center stripe and left/right edge
                     dashes (widths lerped far -> near)
5. End SpriteBatch.


VARIABLE GLOSSARY
-----------------
_pixel
  1x1 white texture used as a "paintbrush" for colored rectangles.

_roadScroll
  How far the road pattern has scrolled in world units. Advances while
  Enter or Space is held; wrapped with modulo so it never grows forever.

SkyColor / GrassColor / RoadColor / CurbColor / LineColor
  Tint colors for sky, grass, road surface, road edges, and center dashes.

SkyHeightFraction
  How much of the screen height is sky (0.4 = top 40%).

FarRoadHalfWidthFraction
  At the horizon: half the road width as a fraction of screen width.
  "Half" because we measure left/right from the center.

NearRoadHalfWidthFraction
  At the bottom of the screen: half the road width as a fraction
  of screen width.

FarCurbWidthPixels / NearCurbWidthPixels
  Black curb thickness in pixels at the horizon (far) and bottom (near).

FarCenterLineHalfWidthPixels / NearCenterLineHalfWidthPixels
  Half-width of the white center dash at the horizon and bottom.

FarSideLineWidthPixels / NearSideLineWidthPixels
  Full width of each white edge dash at the horizon and bottom.

RoadScrollSpeed
  World units per second added to _roadScroll while Enter or Space is held.

DashLength / GapLength
  Length of the painted dash and the empty gap in world units.
  Together they make one stripe period. Keep these small (short dash,
  modest gap) for a dense Pole Position–style center line.

DepthEpsilon
  Small floor for depthFromHorizon so 1/depth never divides by zero
  at the horizon.

horizonRow
  Y pixel where sky meets ground. Road drawing starts here.

groundRowCount
  How many rows exist from the horizon to the bottom of the screen.

roadCenterX
  Horizontal center of the road (middle of the window for a straight road).

farRoadHalfWidthPixels / nearRoadHalfWidthPixels
  Far/near half-road widths converted from fractions into actual pixels
  for this window size.

screenRow
  The current horizontal scanline (Y) being painted in the loop.

depthFromHorizon
  0.0 at the horizon (far), 1.0 at the bottom (near).
  Used to blend widths between far and near.

halfRoadWidth
  Half the gray road width on this screenRow, in pixels.

curbWidth
  Black curb width on this screenRow, in pixels.


DRAWING ONE ROW (around roadCenterX)
------------------------------------
Left curb starts at:  roadCenterX - halfRoadWidth - curbWidth
Gray road starts at:  roadCenterX - halfRoadWidth
                      width = halfRoadWidth * 2
Right curb starts at: roadCenterX + halfRoadWidth
Center dash (if any): roadCenterX - halfLineWidth
                      width = halfLineWidth * 2
Left edge dash:       roadCenterX - halfRoadWidth
                      width = sideLineWidth
Right edge dash:      roadCenterX + halfRoadWidth - sideLineWidth
                      width = sideLineWidth
