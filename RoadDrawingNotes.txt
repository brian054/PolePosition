Pole Position — road drawing notes
=================================

THE TRICK
---------
The road is not one big shape. It is painted one horizontal row at a time
from the horizon down to the bottom of the screen.

Each row draws up to eight short bars around the screen center:
    1) left outer edge (thin black, against the grass)
    2) left rumble curb (red or white)
    3) gray road
    4) right rumble curb (red or white)
    5) right outer edge (thin black)
    6) white center dash (only on short-period dash rows)
    7) yellow left edge dash (inset from the rumble)
    8) yellow right edge dash (inset from the rumble)

Rows near the horizon are narrow (far away).
Rows near the bottom are wide (close to the camera).
That creates the classic trapezoid / perspective look.


SCROLL / INFINITE ROAD
----------------------
Holding Enter or Space ramps _currentSpeed up toward MaxRoadSpeed.
Releasing both keys ramps speed back down to a stop. _roadScroll only
advances while speed is above zero.

Original Pole Position paints every marking from one road texture with
one scroll. We do the same: one _roadScroll, three spatial periods.

For each screen row we map depth to a world Z with:
  worldZ = 1 / max(depthFromHorizon, DepthEpsilon)

Then:
  stripePos = worldZ + _roadScroll

Center line (short period):
  phase = stripePos % (DashLength + GapLength)
  If phase is in the dash half: draw the white center line.

Rumble curb (dense equal red/white):
  rumblePhase = stripePos % (RumbleRedLength + RumbleWhiteLength)
  Paint the curb red or white from that phase. Short equal lengths make
  the red/white blocks tight, like the arcade kerb.

Yellow edge line (longest period):
  yellowPhase = stripePos % (YellowDashLength + YellowGapLength)
  If yellowPhase is in the dash half: draw the yellow inner-edge dashes.
  They sit inward from the rumble so a strip of gray shows between them.

The yellow line is not a second speed. Its dashes and gaps are longer, so
fewer cycles pass per second and it looks slower — matching the arcade.

Because worldZ packs near the horizon, dashes look denser far away
and longer up close — that sells the infinite-road motion.

_roadScroll wraps on the yellow period (the longest one) so all three
patterns stay continuous.


FLOW OF Draw()
--------------
1. Read the window size (viewport).
2. Figure out where the horizon is (horizonRow) from SkyHeightFraction.
3. Clear the screen, then draw the sky band and the full grass band.
4. Loop screenRow from horizonRow to the bottom of the window:
   a. depthFromHorizon = how far down this row is (0 = far, 1 = near)
   b. halfRoadWidth    = lerp far road width -> near road width
   c. curbWidth        = lerp far curb width -> near curb width
   d. outerEdgeWidth   = lerp far outer-edge width -> near outer-edge width
   e. Map depth -> worldZ, add _roadScroll
   f. Decide center dash vs gap, rumble red vs white, and yellow dash vs gap
   g. Draw outer black, rumble, gray road, rumble, outer black
   h. If center dash: draw white center stripe
   i. If yellow dash: draw left/right yellow edge dashes, inset from the rumble
5. Draw the player car rectangle, centered on the road near the bottom.
6. End SpriteBatch.


VARIABLE GLOSSARY
-----------------
_pixel
  1x1 white texture used as a "paintbrush" for colored rectangles.

_roadScroll
  How far the road pattern has scrolled in world units. Advances at
  _currentSpeed; wrapped on the yellow period so it never grows forever
  and all three stripe patterns stay continuous.

_currentSpeed
  Current scroll speed in world units per second. Ramps up while Enter
  or Space is held, ramps down when both are released.

SkyColor / GrassColor / RoadColor / OuterEdgeColor / CurbRed / CurbWhite /
LineColor / YellowLineColor / CarColor
  Tint colors for sky, grass, road surface, thin outer edge, rumble
  curbs, center dashes, yellow edge dashes, and the player car.

SkyHeightFraction
  How much of the screen height is sky (0.4 = top 40%).

FarRoadHalfWidthFraction
  At the horizon: half the road width as a fraction of screen width.
  "Half" because we measure left/right from the center.

NearRoadHalfWidthFraction
  At the bottom of the screen: half the road width as a fraction
  of screen width.

FarCurbWidthPixels / NearCurbWidthPixels
  Red/white rumble thickness in pixels at the horizon (far) and bottom (near).
  Keep these fairly wide so the kerb reads like arcade Pole Position.

FarOuterEdgeWidthPixels / NearOuterEdgeWidthPixels
  Thin black outline outside the rumble at the horizon and bottom.

FarCenterLineHalfWidthPixels / NearCenterLineHalfWidthPixels
  Half-width of the white center dash at the horizon and bottom.

FarSideLineWidthPixels / NearSideLineWidthPixels
  Full width of each yellow edge dash at the horizon and bottom.

FarYellowInsetPixels / NearYellowInsetPixels
  How far the yellow dash sits inward from the rumble, so gray asphalt
  shows between them.

MaxRoadSpeed
  Top scroll speed in world units per second.

RoadAcceleration / RoadDeceleration
  How quickly _currentSpeed climbs toward MaxRoadSpeed while accelerating,
  and how quickly it falls when the keys are released.

DashLength / GapLength
  Length of the painted center dash and the empty gap in world units.
  Together they make the short stripe period used by the center line.
  Keep these small (short dash, modest gap) for a dense Pole Position–style
  center line.

RumbleRedLength / RumbleWhiteLength
  Length of each red and white rumble block. Keep these short and equal so
  the kerb has tight gaps between colors. Around 0.8 each is a fast
  Pole Position–style checker.

YellowDashLength / YellowGapLength
  Length of the yellow edge dash and its gap. Longer than the center period
  so the yellow line appears to move slower.

CarWidthFraction / CarHeightFraction
  Player car size as a fraction of the window width and height.

CarBottomMarginFraction
  How far the car sits above the bottom of the window, as a fraction of
  height, so some road shows under it.

DepthEpsilon
  Small floor for depthFromHorizon so 1/depth never divides by zero
  at the horizon.

horizonRow
  Y pixel where sky meets ground. Road drawing starts here.

groundRowCount
  How many rows exist from the horizon to the bottom of the screen.

roadCenterX
  Horizontal center of the road (middle of the window for a straight road).

farRoadHalfWidthPixels / nearRoadHalfWidthPixels
  Far/near half-road widths converted from fractions into actual pixels
  for this window size.

screenRow
  The current horizontal scanline (Y) being painted in the loop.

depthFromHorizon
  0.0 at the horizon (far), 1.0 at the bottom (near).
  Used to blend widths between far and near.

halfRoadWidth
  Half the gray road width on this screenRow, in pixels.

curbWidth
  Red/white rumble width on this screenRow, in pixels.

outerEdgeWidth
  Thin black outer-edge width on this screenRow, in pixels.


DRAWING ONE ROW (around roadCenterX)
------------------------------------
Left outer edge:      roadCenterX - halfRoadWidth - curbWidth - outerEdgeWidth
                      width = outerEdgeWidth
Left rumble:          roadCenterX - halfRoadWidth - curbWidth
                      width = curbWidth
Gray road starts at:  roadCenterX - halfRoadWidth
                      width = halfRoadWidth * 2
Right rumble:         roadCenterX + halfRoadWidth
                      width = curbWidth
Right outer edge:     roadCenterX + halfRoadWidth + curbWidth
                      width = outerEdgeWidth
Center dash (if any): roadCenterX - halfLineWidth
                      width = halfLineWidth * 2
Left yellow dash:     roadCenterX - halfRoadWidth + yellowInset
                      width = sideLineWidth
Right yellow dash:    roadCenterX + halfRoadWidth - yellowInset - sideLineWidth
                      width = sideLineWidth
